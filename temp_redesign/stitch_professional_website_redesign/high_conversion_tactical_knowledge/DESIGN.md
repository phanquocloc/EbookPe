## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, friction-free digital commerce atmosphere tailored for entrepreneurs, creators, and knowledge seekers. Its primary mission is conversion rate optimization (CRO) paired with immediate intellectual credibility.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Trustworthy & Authoritative:** High legibility, institutional slate tones, and structured micro-copy remove skepticism around digital transactions.
- **Fast & Decisive:** Visual clarity and high-contrast call-to-actions make discovery and checkout intuitive within 3 steps.
- **Modern & Tactical:** A blend of clean tech typography, vibrant primary blues, and warm amber-orange accents that suggest high-velocity execution and tangible value.

### Aesthetic Movement: High-Contrast Corporate / Tech Modern
The style leverages bright ivory and slate canvas tones (`#f8fafc` and `#fffbeb`), ultra-crisp slate typography (`#0f172a`), layered pill badges, and layered atmospheric drop shadows. It avoids flat dullness through soft gradient fills, distinct border lines (`border-slate-200/80`), and focused high-saturation conversion beacons.

## Colors

The palette balances clinical clarity with high-conversion emotional triggers. Light canvas backgrounds keep the reading experience crisp, while saturated primary blues denote technical authority and amber-orange triggers urgency.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Blue (`#0284c7` & `#0369a1`):** Represents institutional confidence, transaction security, and intellectual depth. Used for brand headers, primary utility buttons, active filter states, and step numbers.
- **Accent Orange / Amber (`#f97316`):** The conversion engine. Reserved for direct purchasing triggers (e.g., "Mua ngay", "Xem combo tiết kiệm", limited-time sale badges, and highlight tags). Never overused; its exclusivity maintains click intent.
- **Canvas & Surface Layering (`#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`, `#ffffff`):** Layered background surfaces give breathable separation without dark dividers. A subtle top-of-hero warm tint (`#fffbeb` or `#fefce8`) introduces optimism.
- **Text & High-Contrast Anchors (`#0f172a` & `#334155`):** Text hierarchy relies on rich slate ink to achieve maximum contrast against pure white cards and tinted canvases.

## Typography

Typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across display, body, and label roles. Its geometric roundness combined with crisp vertical terminals produces a high-tech modern aesthetic without sacrificing readability during extended catalog exploration.

### Typographic Rules
- **Headline Tightening:** Display and headline levels utilize negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` and `800`) to guarantee high-impact scanning.
- **Conversion Badges & Sub-labels:** Category chips, trust seals, and value proposition banners use uppercase or title-case `label-badge` styling with subtle positive letter spacing (`0.06em`) to frame headings.
- **Body Rhythm:** Body copy defaults to `#334155` for high contrast that avoids the harshness of pure black while maintaining accessible reading contrast.

## Layout & Spacing

The system enforces a 12-column fixed-max grid centered within a maximum container width of `1200px`. Spacing distances follow a consistent 8-point base rhythm, balancing density for catalogue browsing with spaciousness in conversion hero zones.

### Breakpoint Matrix
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) outer margins. Cards display in 3- or 4-column configurations.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `1.5rem` (24px) outer margins. Cards re-flow to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) outer margins. Action buttons span full width; filter chips shift to horizontally scrollable rails.

### Section Vertical Cadence
- Hero & feature introduction blocks: `space-xl` (40px to 64px) top and bottom margins.
- Card internals: `space-lg` (24px) padding on desktop, `space-md` (16px) padding on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered ambient shadows, subtle surface borders, and elevated floating containers that pull focus toward checkout funnels.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Surface color `#f8fafc` or soft gradient `#f8fafc` transitioning to `#f1f5f9`.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Cards & Pills):** White surfaces (`#ffffff`) with a hairline border (`1px solid #e2e8f0`) and an ambient glow:
  - `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Sticky Navigation):** Used for Ebook product cards, step progress indicators, and filter bars:
  - `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (High-Conversion CTAs & Hover States):** Used for primary conversion triggers and floating notification toasts:
  - `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.25)` for Primary Blue buttons.
  - `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.35)` for Accent Orange buttons.

## Shapes

The interface balances soft friendliness with structured precision using a dual radius philosophy:

- **Cards & Major Content Blocks:** Enforce `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / 16px to `1.25rem` / 20px). This provides large, gentle corners that frame book covers and benefit highlights cleanly.
- **Buttons & Text Inputs:** Styled with `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) to communicate strong touch targets and click confidence.
- **Pills, Trust Badges & Floating Social Proof:** Use fully rounded pill geometries (`9999px`) to distinguish meta status indicators from actionable rectangular elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion Button (Orange):** Solid `#f97316` fill, `#ffffff` text, bold weight (`600`), `rounded-xl`, with a warm ambient shadow. On hover: transitions to `#ea580c` with vertical lift (`translateY(-2px)`).
- **Secondary Action Button (Blue):** Solid `#0284c7` fill, `#ffffff` text, `rounded-xl`. On hover: `#0369a1`.
- **Ghost / Neutral Outline Button:** Pure white background, `border border-slate-200`, `#0f172a` text, hovering to `bg-slate-50 border-slate-300`.

### Badges & Category Chips
- **Category Filter Chips:** Pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.5rem 1rem`. Inactive state: `bg-white`, `text-slate-600`, hairline `border-slate-200`. Active state: `bg-sky-600`, `text-white`, shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.25)`.
- **Social Proof / Live Toasts:** Pill container with white backdrop, subtle border, containing a pulsing green or blue status dot, bold username highlight, and muted timestamp (`#64748b`).
- **Feature Badges:** Translucent sky-tinted pill (`bg-sky-50 text-sky-700 border border-sky-100`) with high-contrast text and star/sparkle icon prefixes.

### Cards
- **Product & Benefit Cards:** Pure white surface (`#ffffff`), `rounded-2xl`, border `1px solid #f1f5f9`, multi-tier ambient shadow. Benefit cards feature a centered or top-left icon inside a softly tinted squircle container (`rounded-xl` with `bg-sky-50` or `bg-amber-50`).
- **Hover State:** Transform lift (`-4px`) paired with a deeper, slightly tinted blue shadow to signal interactivity.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search Bar:** Height `48px`, `rounded-xl`, pure white background with an inner icon prefix (`#94a3b8`), `1px solid #e2e8f0` border, expanding slightly or gaining a `ring-2 ring-sky-500/20 border-sky-500` outline on focus.

### Step Indicator Process
- **Process Line:** Horizontal connector line in `#e2e8f0` with active section filled in `#0284c7`.
- **Step Node:** Crisp circular avatar (`48px x 48px`), white fill, `2px solid #0284c7`, containing bold step indices (`1`, `2`, `3`) in primary blue.
